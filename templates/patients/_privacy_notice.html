Explain privacy before collecting details. Show or read the Patient Privacy and Data-Sharing Notice to the patient before entering their phone number or other details. Ask for their agreement to the requested lookup and registration; do not proceed if they decline.

Explain that associated vendors can access the complete Baton BP and follow-up history. The Privacy Policy gives more detail. Reading the notice or providing an OTP is not consent.

Ask about SMS and voice consent separately. Either may be declined without preventing registration or record access. The patient can withdraw either choice through an associated vendor or the project contact.
