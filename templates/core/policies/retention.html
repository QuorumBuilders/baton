What the application currently does

Patient, BP, follow-up, association and communication records remain in the local database; there is no automated patient-data deletion schedule. OTPs expire and become unusable, but expiry does not itself delete verification records. Django sessions expire independently; removal of expired stored sessions needs the standard session-cleanup operation, which has not been configured as a scheduled task.

Saved BP measurements have no ordinary edit or delete operation. Follow-up revisions, original measurement vendors, association confirmations and communication choices are preserved as history. Deactivating a vendor account or withdrawing reminders does not automatically delete these records. This implementation preserves history; it does not establish a legal entitlement to retain everything forever.

Access and correction

Ask an associated vendor or use Contact and Privacy Complaints to request access or a correction. Requests should identify the issue without unnecessary medical details in an initial email. Identity and authority must be checked before disclosing or changing records.

Patient demographic information can be reviewed by an authorized administrator. Errors in saved BP or follow-up history need a reviewed correction process that preserves the original entry and makes the correction clear. The prototype has no dedicated historical correction interface; measurements must not be silently overwritten to hide an error.

Restriction, deletion and withdrawal

You may request restricted processing, object where applicable, withdraw consent, or request deletion under applicable law. Such requests must be assessed individually against the purpose, legal basis, accuracy concerns, applicable obligations and any necessary retention for legal claims. A justified need to preserve a historical record is not an automatic refusal of every deletion request.

The operator should explain the decision, any lawful reason for retaining particular information and any restriction applied. A production process must cover associated vendors and copies held by service providers, logs and backups where applicable. No automatic erasure, anonymisation, sharing-removal or restriction workflow is implemented here; a reviewed operational procedure is still required.

Routine SMS or voice withdrawal can be recorded immediately by an associated vendor through the patient record and prevents future routine invocations through that channel. Withdrawal does not undo prior lawful processing. Requests about the underlying health-record agreement or vendor sharing should be handled separately, with lawful access restriction where required.

Complaints

If you have concerns about retention or a request decision, use the project contact or complain directly to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. These pages do not limit your statutory rights.
