Baton is a record-keeping and follow-up coordination platform. It stores BP measurements entered by vendors and coordinates visit and general-health reminders.

  • Baton does not diagnose hypertension or interpret BP measurements.
  • It does not prescribe medication, recommend treatment or specify dosage.
  • It does not provide clinical decision support or replace a qualified medical professional.
  • Medication reminders repeat the intention to follow instructions already given by a healthcare professional. They do not change those instructions.

Automatic follow-up dates are scheduling intervals, not an assessment of your medical condition. An overdue flag tells a vendor that a scheduled follow-up needs attention; it is not a diagnosis or clinical risk assessment.

Seek appropriate professional care for BP questions, symptoms, medication questions or other medical concerns. For an urgent concern, seek prompt medical help; do not wait for a Baton reminder. The prototype does not deliver real SMS or voice calls and is not an emergency service.
